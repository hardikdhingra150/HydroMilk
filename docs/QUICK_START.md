# HydraMilk — your whole month in one place

**Month** is the new main screen. Choose a month and year at the top. Every date is listed, with **Milk** and **Water** side by side.

- Each recorded cell shows quantity, charge, and **Paid / Part paid / Unpaid** status.
- **No entry** means nothing was recorded. It is not an assumed delivery or a skipped day.
- **Not received** means you explicitly recorded that nothing arrived; it costs zero.
- Tap a milk/water cell to review that date or add a delivery for that exact day. Tap the date to review both services. Multiple deliveries on a day are combined in the register; the detail panel shows each one and lets you edit it.
- Monthly cards show total milk litres, water cans, charges, paid amount, and balance.

Use **+ Milk / + Water** for a new entry. Check the full delivery date, choose/type quantity, enter the price, and save. Manual dates use **DD/MM/YYYY**. Use **Entry list** when you want individual records and Paid/Unpaid filters.

Open **Bills** after paying a supplier. **Mark paid** fills the remaining amount; change it for a part payment. Check the payment date, then save. Payments cover the oldest deliveries within that supplier's billing month. Incorrect payment records can be removed from Payment history. HydraMilk records payments; it does not transfer money.

For sharing, the first person signs in and creates a family, then shares the code from **Family**. Others sign in and choose **Join a family**. Shared records refresh automatically while active, after writes, and when reopening. Use the refresh icon if needed. Internet is required for shared records.

The export icon on Month shares a CSV with the entire daily register, individual entries, payments, and totals. Date/year selection also works for earlier months.

Install **HydraMilk-v3.1.apk** over the earlier app to keep its records. Fresh installations have no sample bills. Local mode stays separate from a shared family; joining an existing family loads its real records. Export important records before uninstalling or clearing app data.

## Set reminders on each phone

After installing the update, open HydraMilk and tap the bell at the top. Turn on **Daily entry reminder**, allow Android notifications, and choose a time. You can also enable **Monthly bill review** for the 1st. Tap **Send a test notification** and check the notification tray. Each family member enables reminders on their own phone.
